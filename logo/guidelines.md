# runherd — Logo Guidelines (compact)

## 1. Logo
- **Idea**: ring observasi + satelit lepas — sesi diawasi tenang, biaya tercatat presisi.
- **Versions**: horizontal (`runherd-horizontal*.svg`) · stacked (`runherd-stacked.svg`) · symbol (`runherd-symbol.svg`) · small-size (`runherd-symbol-small.svg`, `runherd-symbol-favicon.svg`).
- **Masters**: `logo/*.svg`. Warna penuh = simbol teal + wordmark ink.

## 2. Clear space
Zona kosong **1 × diameter dot satelit** di semua sisi. Skala ikut logo.

## 3. Minimum size
| Version | Screen | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal | ≥96px lebar | di bawah itu pakai simbol |
| Symbol | ≥16px | pakai `-favicon` / `-small` di ≤32px |

## 4. Colour
| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK | Pantone approx |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Herd Teal | #0F766E | 15 118 110 | 87 0 7 54 | 7474 C |
| Ink | #111111 | 17 17 17 | — | Black 7 C |

**Pairs**: teal+ink di putih · putih di Herd Teal · putih di Ink · hitam di putih. Foto: putih/hitam di area tenang.

## 5. Typography
- Wordmark eksplorasi: Inter SemiBold lowercase, tracking -2%.
- **Final trademark wajib outline wordmark ke paths** (audit sekarang FAIL live-text by design).
- Body/UI: system stack. Lisensi: Inter OFL aman.

## 6. Don'ts
No stretch · no recolour · no rotate · no shadow/gradient · no susun ulang lockup · no busy background tanpa container · no ketik ulang wordmark.

## 7. Files
`logo/runherd-symbol{,-black,-white,-mono-0f766e,-square,-favicon,-app-icon}.svg`, `runherd-horizontal{-black,-white,-mono-0f766e}.svg`, `preview.html`, `presentation.html`.
